Summary

Business sponsorEveryone

A voice agent that answers disruption calls, rebooks what it can, and hands the rest to a person who already knows the story.

The problem

Peak demand, slowest path

A storm cancels flights and calls jump 20x in minutes. Callers queue, repeat their booking reference, and every call loads a reservation system with strict rate limits.

The solution

A voice agent at the front door

It identifies the caller, explains their options, then rebooks them with an SMS confirmation, or hands off to a Genesys agent with full context. When all agents are busy, it books a callback.

What stays

Nothing is ripped out

Twilio keeps the numbers, Genesys keeps routing and the human agents, and the reservation system stays the system of record. If the bot is down, the call goes to the human queue.

Architecture

BusinessIT architect

Twilio is the carrier, Genesys is the front door, and Zingly is a modular, room-based voice agent platform behind it.

How a call flows

Step numbers refer to Figure 1.

  1. The caller dials the hotline. Twilio owns the number and passes the call to Genesys (steps 1–2).
  2. A Genesys inbound flow decides bot or human, using the A/B traffic percentage between the legacy IVR and Zingly. For the bot share, it calls Zingly's gateway to create a conversation (step 3).
  3. Zingly creates a LiveKit room and returns the room ID. Genesys joins the room by SIP, and the voice agent worker joins too: both are participants in one room.
  4. The worker handles speech and streams to the STT/TTS vendor. The welcome and consent audio is pre-rendered and cached, so it plays without a TTS call.
  5. Transcripts go to the AI-routing layer: welcome, consent, disambiguation, case management, routing. It calls the separate Auth service when a request needs verification (step 5).
  6. A supervisor agent picks a domain agent: Rebooking, Flight Status or FAQ (step 7). Domain agents reach the LLMs only through the AI gateway, using EU-region deployments of OpenAI and Anthropic.
  7. Every tool call passes the Policy engine (guardrails, resilience, rate limiting), then the MCP server, which reaches Atlantica's APIs, databases and knowledge base (step 8).
  8. When the bot finishes or escalates, it returns conversation_status=AGENT_HANDOVER with category, subcategory and summary, and Genesys routes to a dedicated human agent.

Why this shape

Genesys in front gives A/B rollout, queues, callbacks and the human fallback for free. LiveKit rooms make the bot a real participant, so a human can join the same room, and turn-taking, barge-in and vendor swaps come from the framework. Layered agents mean a new use case, such as baggage, is just a new domain agent. The AI gateway handles model routing, failover and PII redaction, and the MCP server is one standard door to Atlantica systems.

Voice pipeline

IT architect

Zingly sits behind Genesys, which sits behind Twilio. Twilio is only the carrier. Every stage streams, and a caller can say anything.

Zingly receives the call from Genesys over SIP, into a LiveKit room.

Barge-in is handled in the voice agent worker. At least 200 ms of caller speech plus a speech-to-text partial stops the audio, trims the bot's turn to the words actually heard, and tells the router. The AI disclosure and itinerary read-back cannot be interrupted.

Question

How is "Hi" handled without an LLM?

A fast path sits in front of the model. It normalises the transcript and compares it to short lists of greetings, acknowledgements, yes/no words and keypad digits. It fires only when the whole utterance matches, so "Hi, my flight was cancelled" goes on to the agent. Yes and no are read against the pending question the session holds (consent, or "shall I book the 14:05?"), so they are never guessed. The welcome already played at call start, so the reply is a cached phrase. Anything unmatched goes to the LLM: a miss costs one call, not a wrong answer.

Question

How do we know the caller stayed on topic?

We check on every turn at no extra cost. The domain agent runs every turn anyway, and its output begins with a small decision field: stay, switch:<domain> or clarify. Speech starts only after that field, so nothing off-topic is spoken. On a topic change the small classifier confirms the new domain, and session state (identity, booking, pending choice) carries over so the first task can resume. A side question goes to the FAQ agent and then returns to the open task.

Failure path

After three failed attempts self-service locks for one hour. The caller still gets public information or a person, and the agent is told the caller was not verified. The bot never confirms that a booking reference exists.

Who can rebook by voice?

Only a Verified caller, within policy, for the passengers on that booking.

Why not authenticate every call upfront in Genesys? It adds friction for every caller, including those who only want flight status. Booking references mix letters and digits, which is awkward on a keypad. And it duplicates what the bot already does in conversation.

Protecting the reservation system

IT architect

A storm makes callers correlated: a thousand people on AT123 want the same alternatives. So we keep a copy per flight, ask the PSS once for many callers, and queue the rest by priority.

Step 1

Prepare early

A flight.cancelled event triggers a worker that pulls the passenger list once and computes alternatives before most callers dial.

Step 2

Keep a copy

Zingly's own encrypted copy of status, passenger list and options. It is not a PSS replica.

Step 3

Ask once

Concurrent requests for the same thing become one PSS query.

1,000 callers → 1 query
Step 4

Priority lanes

Requests that miss the copy queue by importance, so a status lookup never blocks a rebooking.

Limit never exceeded
LaneShareWhen full, the caller gets
P0 · Book or hold a seat40%Deferred commit: choice recorded, booked shortly after, SMS confirmation (needs business sign-off)
P1 · Find the caller's booking30%A snapshot labelled "as of 14:02"
P2 · Refresh options20%The indicative copy, re-checked live before booking
P3 · Flight status10%The saved copy only

Safe to keep: flight status (30 s safety limit), passenger list (until the flight closes), options (20–30 s, offer only), a booking (for the call only). Never kept: holds and commits. A booking always re-checks the PSS live.

At 20x. Genesys asks a capacity check on Zingly before routing each call, which reads active sessions, vendor headroom and reservation-system health. A predictive autoscaler adds voice workers when a disruption event arrives, before the calls do, and scales on active sessions per worker rather than CPU. Speech and LLM vendors have reserved capacity and a secondary. Above capacity, the call overflows to the human queue with a callback offer, so no caller gets a busy tone.

Genesys handover, screen-pop & callback

BusinessIT architect

Genesys owns the queue, the wait time and the callback. The agent's screen opens already filled in.

The bot returns AGENT_HANDOVER with category, subcategory and summary, who the caller is and how they were verified, the booking and disruption, and every attempted action with its result, for example "rebook to AT127 failed: reservation timeout, booking unchanged". The Genesys flow resumes and reads these attributes. Sensitive detail is fetched by a Data Action under OAuth rather than stored in attributes.

Short wait

Skill queue, then screen-pop

Routed by language, tier and intent. The agent sees who, why and what was tried.

Long wait or all agents busy

Callback that keeps the place

The flow offers it, an SMS confirms it, and the agent gets the same screen-pop with the same attributes.

Integration & security

IT architectSecurity

Every hop is authenticated with a short-lived, audience-bound token. Writes are idempotent and never retried blindly. Passenger data stays in the EU.

Retries & idempotency

  • Rebooking key = hash(tenant, booking, from-flight, to-flight). It excludes the call ID, so a dropped call that dials back cannot rebook twice.
  • A timed-out write is never blindly retried: read the booking first, then retry under the same key.
  • A failed commit after a hold releases the hold.

Data protection

  • Residency: the whole platform, including the OpenAI and Anthropic deployments, runs in the EU. A DPA with SCCs and a transfer-impact assessment are still needed, as Zingly is a US vendor.
  • PII is tokenised before the model and restored at speech time. No payment or health data reaches it. No retention, no training.
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest with per-tenant keys. Redacted transcripts kept 30 days, handoff context 24 hours, no audio stored.

Model safety: flight times and entitlements come from tools and approved wording, never the model. Tools are allow-listed per identity level and every call is logged.

Compliance. GDPR / UK GDPR is the primary frame (contract performance, data minimisation, DPIA before go-live).
